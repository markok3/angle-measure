Angle Measure

Privacy Policy

Effective 29 October 2026

Angle Measure is a measuring app for iPhone made by Marko Angelovski. This policy explains what the app does with information. In short: it collects nothing.

What we collect

Nothing. Angle Measure has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. It does not send any information to us or to anyone else.

What the app uses on your phone

  • Hinge angle and motion sensors. The app reads the hinge angle, accelerometer, gyroscope, magnetometer and barometer to show measurements. These readings are processed on your phone, shown on screen and not stored.
  • Location. Only if you tap “Use location” and allow it. Your location is used on your phone to point the compass at true north and to show your coordinates, speed and altitude. It is never sent anywhere or stored.
  • Motion & Fitness. Only if you tap “Turn on” and allow it. Step counts, floors, activity and air pressure are read from your phone to show them on screen. They are never sent anywhere or stored by the app.
  • Settings. Your choices, such as units, north reference and the last tool you used, are saved on your phone. They are deleted when you delete the app.

You can turn location or Motion & Fitness access off at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security.

Children

The app collects no information from anyone, including children.
